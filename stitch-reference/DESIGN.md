## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intimacy, heritage, and relaxed warmth of a modern Indian parlor house ("Baithak"). The aesthetic pairs refined European coffeehouse traditions with warm Indian hospitality, invoking slow mornings, lingering conversations, and aromatic roasted spices and coffee beans.

The core visual style is **Contemporary Warm Tactile**:
- High-touch warmth built upon ivory canvases and rich mahogany-maroon accents.
- Soft, inviting layer transitions that mimic fine stationary, steamed porcelain, and polished wood surfaces.
- Atmospheric restraint: uncluttered layouts prioritizing spacious editorial pacing, delicate typographic hierarchy, and warm organic depth over harsh digital contrast.

## Colors

The palette relies on organic, culinary-inspired tonal values rather than stark digital primaries:

- **Primary (`#922F3E`) & Deep Wine (`#702331`):** Applied to core interactive drivers, highlighted badges, key brand statements, and hero iconography.
- **Secondary Warm Wood (`#8B6245`):** Expresses artisanal craftsmanship, roasting notes, and architectural wood tones. Used for secondary actions, subtle hover states, and graphic accents.
- **Ivory (`#FFFDF8`) & Cream (`#F7F1E6`):** Establish the soft, glare-free canvas surfaces, substituting raw digital white with the tactility of handmade paper and steamed milk.
- **Deep Espresso (`#302722`) & Muted Taupe (`#786F68`):** Replace pure black for all typography, ensuring reading comfort with high legibility against the cream grounds.

## Typography

The typography couples the editorial romance of high-contrast transitional serifs with modern geometric sans-serif utility.

- **Headlines:** Use Playfair Display with deliberate optical tracking to reflect vintage menu typographics, curated tasting notes, and comfortable storytelling.
- **Body & Labels:** Use Plus Jakarta Sans to maintain effortless legibility for long-form culinary descriptions, item pricing, nutrition details, and navigation tags.
- Use title case for primary headings, and subtle uppercase tracking for small sub-headers, dietary flags, and price tiers.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive grid underpins desktop screens, transforming to a 6-column grid on tablet and a 4-column flow on mobile. 

- Maintain expansive outer section margins (`space-xl` to multiple tiers) to allow menu items and imagery room to breathe.
- Coffee cards and tasting menus follow a balanced 3-column desktop layout, folding into a 2-column grid on tablet, and single/horizontal snap-scroll carousels on mobile.
- Use vertical rhythm aligned strictly to 4px and 8px step multiples to balance photographic assets and dense menu ingredient listings.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on physical layering and warm ambient diffusion rather than standard drop shadows:

- **Surface Tonal Layers:** The base page renders on Warm Cream (`#F7F1E6`). Elevated containers (such as menu cards, modal dialogue sheets, and navigation bars) use Surface Ivory (`#FFFDF8`).
- **Warm Ambient Shadows:** Shadows are warm-tinted and deeply diffused:
  - *Resting card:* `0 4px 18px -2px rgba(48, 39, 34, 0.05), 0 1px 3px rgba(48, 39, 34, 0.03)`
  - *Floating / Hover:* `0 12px 30px -4px rgba(48, 39, 34, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(112, 35, 49, 0.04)`
- **Low-Contrast Borders:** Structural definition is reinforced by subtle borders tinted with `#E7DCcb` at 1px width, creating a tactile stationery feel without severe delineation.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a curated, softened profile:

- Base interactive items (buttons, standard inputs, cards) use Soft roundedness (`0.25rem` to `0.5rem`), referencing vintage book covers, hand-cut menu inserts, and porcelain tableware.
- Decorative image frames and feature product containers use `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem`) or custom arched top treatments (`rounded-t-full`) for hero café spotlights.
- Pills and circular shapes are reserved strictly for dietary labels (e.g., Vegan, Single Origin), floating action buttons, and numeric quantity adjusters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled with Warm Burgundy (`#922F3E`), text in Surface Ivory (`#FFFDF8`), soft radius (`0.25rem`), padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Hover state deepens to Dark Wine (`#702331`) with a subtle `2px` upward translation.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Ivory fill with Warm Wood Brown (`#8B6245`) outline and text. Hover applies a soft cream wash.
- **Text Action:** Espresso text (`#302722`) with an animated bottom underline in Warm Burgundy.

### Coffee & Menu Cards
- Ivory background (`#FFFDF8`) with a fine hairline border (`#E7DCcb`), resting on subtle warm ambient shadow.
- Image aspect ratio of 4:3 or 1:1 with soft corner radiuses.
- Card contents include: Playfair Display title, origin/flavor badge, concise tasting notes in body-sm, and price placed in bold Espresso text alongside an add-to-order button.

### Chips & Tags
- Compact height (`28px`), muted taupe text on a soft cream background (`#EFE8DC`).
- Active or specialty origin tags use Dark Wine background with Ivory text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: Surface Ivory (`#FFFDF8`). Border: 1px `#E7DCcb`.
- Focus state: Subtle 1px border shift to Warm Burgundy (`#922F3E`) with an ambient wine glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(146, 47, 62, 0.12)`).
- Typography: Deep Espresso text with Muted Taupe placeholder copy.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square with soft 2px corners (checkbox) or circular (radio), styled in 1.5px border `#8B6245`.
- Selected state fills with Warm Burgundy (`#922F3E`) featuring an ivory check or dot.

### Specialized Component: Tasting Flight Selector
- Horizontal linked segments with wood-brown dividers, showing beverage profile graphs (roast level, acidity, milk pairing) using warm-toned micro-steppers.
